Solyra OS

A Linux distro built for humans and AI agents on the same desktop — beautiful, tiny, fast, and forgiving: nothing an agent does is unrecoverable.

In the works · Phase 1
Small: ≤ 4 GB installed, ≤ 400 MiB idle RAM, boots in seconds.
Beautiful: scrollable tiling, snap layouts, one coherent design language.
For agents: their own desktop space, sandboxed, every action auditable and undoable.
For everyone: opinionated defaults, deep customization, gaming ready.
